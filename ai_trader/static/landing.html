From fourteen hundred headlines to one decision.

The agents do the part that does not scale — reading everything, every day, without getting bored or scared. The judgment that decides your money stays with you.

01

They read the market

Overnight, a pool of agents pulls filings, wire coverage, price and volume history, and retail chatter for every name in the watch universe.

02

They argue it down to a ranking

Each name gets scored on evidence strength, not volume of mentions. A story repeated by forty accounts still counts once.

03

You get one call, not a feed

One card. The ticker, the size, the reasoning, and the evidence behind it. Approve and it routes to your brokerage. Skip and nothing happens — which is the default.

It cannot trade without your tap.

Most of the engineering here went into what the system refuses to do. These are enforced in code, not promised in a policy page.

Explicit approval

Every order needs your tap. The default action is always to do nothing, and a missed notification costs you nothing.

A hard budget ceiling

You set the maximum an order can ever be. The agents cannot size past it, and nothing in the loop can raise it on its own.

Long-only equities

No options, no crypto, no shorting, no leverage, no penny stocks. The instruments that turn a bad call into a catastrophe are simply absent.

A full audit trail

Every decision is logged with the evidence that produced it, then scored against what actually happened. You can always ask why.

Read less. Decide better.

The research engine and the approval loop are running today in the dashboard. The phone app is still being built.
